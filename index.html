Process

How a shipment moves

Three stages, one team responsible for all of them — from our consolidation hubs in China to your door in Australia.

01

Pickup & export clearance

Cargo is collected and consolidated at our hubs in Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Dongguan, Foshan or Zhongshan, then declared for export.

02

Transport & tracking

Your shipment moves by sea or air with a reference number you can check in with us at any point.

03

Australian clearance & delivery

We handle customs clearance on arrival and deliver to your door — no separate broker, no extra calls for you to make.

Why East Inter

Built around the Australia route

Since 2011. One of the first China freight forwarders to run a dedicated Australia door-to-door line.

Two offices, one team. Based in Guangzhou with our own people in Sydney and Melbourne.

FCL or LCL. Ship a whole container or share space on ours — same clearance either way.

Duty-exemption help. Support with the paperwork for personal effects when you're moving to Australia.

Five consolidation hubs. Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Dongguan, Foshan and Zhongshan.

Direct sailing, no transshipment. Fewer handoffs, fewer places for a shipment to sit and wait.

Flat-rate pricing. One quoted price, no hidden fees added after booking.

Insured in transit. Cargo insurance through China Pacific Insurance, plus our own cover beyond the policy.

Full-process handling. Export declaration, booking, loading and destination clearance — all under one roof.
